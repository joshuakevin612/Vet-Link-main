The problem

Every second counts, and most tags don't say much.

When a pet goes missing, whoever finds it usually has no way to identify it quickly or reach the right person. A name and a phone number stamped on metal is often all a finder has to go on.

There's no record of vaccinations, medications, or medical history — information a shelter or vet may urgently need, and information an owner shouldn't have to repeat from memory every time.

Have you seen me?

Old tags carry a name and a number. If the finder can't reach the owner, that's where the trail ends.

Get a tag

Pet records live
behind your account.

Create an account, confirm your email, and your dashboard opens. Register a pet there and Vet Link generates a QR code you can print, engrave, or clip to a collar.

Scanning that code shows a stranger only what helps the pet: its name, allergies, medications, conditions, and a button that calls you. Your email and address stay in your account.
